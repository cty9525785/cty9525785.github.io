Tianyu ChenResearch Associate, Bristol Robotics Laboratory

I design robotic structures, from simulation to working hardware.

At the Bristol Robotics Laboratory, University of Bristol, I work in the VIVO Hub for Enhanced Independent Living on electrostatic actuators, a lightweight replacement for the bulky motors and pneumatic systems in exosuits.

Previously, I did my PhD at Nanyang Technological University in Prof. Yifan Wang’s Robotic Materials Group, where I developed a scale-inspired layered structure whose inversely designed surface patterns let it morph into a target shape and change its stiffness at the same time. From it, I built an amphibious robot and a drone landing gear. Before that, I used finite element models to study how thin films and nanostructured metals deform.
