Tianyu Chen

I design and build robots, guided by finite element simulation.

I’m a research associate at the Bristol Robotics Laboratory, University of Bristol, in the VIVO Hub for Enhanced Independent Living, where I research electrostatic actuators as a lightweight replacement for the bulky motors and pneumatic systems in exosuits. Previously, I did my PhD at Nanyang Technological University in Prof. Yifan Wang’s Robotic Materials Group, where I developed a scale-inspired layered structure whose inversely designed surface patterns let it morph into a target shape and change its stiffness at the same time, and built an amphibious robot and a drone landing gear from it. Before that, I used finite element models to study how thin films and nanostructured metals deform.
